# Fony · illustrated Cookie Wall skin

First illustrated skin pass created 6 November 2026 to the Cookie Wall build guide, with the requested toy-like 1990s arcade shapes and neon cyberpunk court. The lock, linked-node, eye, and X marks replace banner lettering; the full names remain in the settings legend. The 34 logical-size PNG frames and one 320×320 WebP court in sprites/ are the live assets. Banner tiles are 34×18; blocker 64×16; packet 12×12; tracker 32×24; debris 8×8.

The renderer animates banner breaks, blocker hits, packet spins, tracker catches, and debris. Full generated sheets and the contact-sheet review are included in the Cookie Wall art review ZIP. Frames are licensed CC BY 4.0 under the attribution in LICENSE and skin.json.
