# Grind the Terms · Fony Sprock (v0.9.2 Consent barrier + grind poses)

> Nobody reads them. Skate them.

The side-scrolling browser runner for Punk Arcade's CH 05. The `arcade` branch uses the approved Fony atlas and punk-comic city art, with a standalone low ACCEPT ALL curb and separate taller consent-form hazards. Desktop stays 16:9; mobile keeps the portrait tablet layout for the ZERO GAME THING player. Gameplay and collision rules remain independent of the image layers.

## Current controls and rules (v0.9)

| Move | Desktop | ZERO GAME THING tablet / touch | Gamepad |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ollie / hold to grind | ↑ / W / U | Hold U | A |
| Pop off a grind | Release ↑ / W / U | Release U | Release A |
| Trick | SPACE or A | A button | B |
| Spray-can rocket | R | Ø button | X |
| Pause / resume | P / Esc or pause button | Pause / Resume buttons | — |

**Grind:** Press U / ↑ / W for one ollie. Hold it as Fony descends to catch a rail or obstacle top, then release to pop off. During the grind, both feet stay on the board. The pop animation ends before atlas pose 34, which has a rail embedded in its art. A tap on the game canvas does not jump, and jump input never triggers a double jump.

**Tricks:** SPACE / desktop A / tablet A performs one trick during an airtime, including after an ollie or a grind pop. It cannot be used while supported or grinding. Trick height is fixed so tricks do not replace the ollie. Score improves the trick name and points: Kickflip (+2), Heelflip (+4) from 50 clauses, and 360 Flip (+6) from 125 clauses. At 80 clauses a glowing trick token appears; collecting it unlocks one extra trick before the next landing. That second combo has a more complex animation and doubles the trick points. Landing resets the airtime trick count; the upgrade stays unlocked for the run.

**Coins and upgrades:** Ground pickups can be collected by rolling through them. Other coins and powerups sit in jump arcs, and a few align with obstacle tops to reward deliberate platform landings. High lines require an ollie. Coins add 10 points. The 80-clause trick token adds the second trick. Spray-can pickups begin at 160 clauses and recur at 120-clause intervals, up to three cans carried.

**Spray-can rocket:** R / Ø uses one collected can for exactly 2× the current normal speed for 3.75 seconds. Each additional can adds another 3.75 seconds, up to 11.25 seconds total with three cans. Rocket boost cannot be activated during a grind; a can stays in inventory until used. A long grind still builds a separate brief +7% momentum bonus.

## Obstacles and scores

- **ACCEPT ALL obstacle:** A low, standalone button curb appears on the beginner route. It has no consent heading, checkbox form, or terms copy; its flat top can be ridden or ground across, and a side impact causes a bail.
- **Consent popup / terms modal:** Separate hazards appear from tiers 2 and 4. The squat POPUP uses the wide CONSENT barrier art (52 units high); the distinct MODAL remains a tall paper form (102 units high). Neither is the standalone ACCEPT ALL curb, and both have rideable tops.
- **Clause rails:** Fewer rails appear in the route; the first is a safe practice rail, with another later for continued grind practice.
- **Fine-print gaps:** Jump the black pit (reward currently +8; balance candidate).
- **Opt Out / Air Boost:** +10 and one-hit protection. They are defensive tokens, not extra jumps.
- **Ordinary coins:** Bright sidewalk-height pickups are placed in trails and gaps between hazards to reward rolling through the line.

## Build and embed

From `arcade/`: `npm install && npm run check && npm run e2e`.

```html
<script type="module" src="./games/grind-the-terms/dist/grind-the-terms.js"></script>
<grind-the-terms skin="./games/grind-the-terms/skins/au/skin.json"></grind-the-terms>
```

`skin` is optional. Without it, the game renders accessible procedural placeholders. Invalid or inaccessible skin assets trigger `game:error` and fall back to placeholders; change the attribute to swap skins without editing gameplay. `game:skin-loaded`, `game:power`, `game:grind-start`, `game:grind-end`, `game:near-miss`, `game:over`, and `game:share` are emitted as DOM events.

**AU art is all rights reserved.** All skater and hazard frames in `skins/au/` are protected branded artwork. The open `skins/fony/` remains placeholder-only until clean, unbranded sprite art is available. Do not redistribute the AU skin under CC BY.

See `GAP-ANALYSIS.md` for the gap-by-gap status, release blockers, and next QA steps.

Sound effects are procedurally generated by Web Audio after a user gesture (disabled by default); enable with the Sound toggle or **M**. No prerecorded music is distributed.

## Rail sprite contact calibration (v0.2.2)

The AU skin declares `rail.drawTopFromGround=39`, `rail.drawHeight=40`, and `rail.surfaceCellY=53` (out of `grid.hazards.cellH=112`). ## Flat-topped obstacles (v0.2.3)

Fony can land on, skate across, and ollie from ACCEPT, POPUP and MODAL boxes. **Hold** while landing for a grind; **release** to pop off. If the jump is released before landing, the box acts as a rideable platform and the next tap triggers a new ollie. Running into its side still causes a bail. The short Consent popup is a 144-unit platform introduced in tier 2 after the beginner practice rail. The physical contact edge is visibly highlighted on the art. Taller drone hazards and elevated rewards are future encounter work, not yet shipped.

## v0.2.4: obstacle artwork grounded to the floor

Restored the original **ACCEPT ALL** sprite with no artificial floating lime coping line. The sprite's actual face is the grindable surface. ACCEPT, POPUP, and MODAL art now anchor their visible bottoms to the floor using source-cell measurements: ACCEPT surface 45/base 92/height 72, popup 24/105/94, modal 27/101/130 (source atlas cell height 112). ## November 4 — v0.5.1 FPS-performance correction

The user clarified **browser animation was stuttering**, not that world-speed was too low. Rolled back the attempted 260 px/s gameplay tuning to the earlier 180 px/s progression (still with the optional 2× speed toggle), restoring the safe beginner window. The source of jank was excessive repeated drawing of large scenic tiles plus text-heavy SVG cloud assets on every animation frame. Cloud SVGs are now lightweight procedural curved paper strata with a small repeating microprint pattern. The game rasterizes each cloud SVG **once when the skin loads** and draws that cached canvas each frame. Parallax draws only cropped visible camera regions, instead of multiple full 1280px background tiles. On touch devices, the Grind canvas resolution is limited to 1.5× DPR instead of 2×, reducing fill pixels while retaining crisp ink sprites. This change has separate regression coverage for visible-only rendering and updated source cropping. Performance still requires a real iPhone run before final acceptance.
