# Grind the Terms · Fony Sprock (M1 mechanics + M2 art preview)

> Nobody reads them. Skate them.

The side-scrolling browser runner for Punk Arcade's CH 05. This branch includes the user-approved **protected AU art preview**, the original Fony character model and paper backgrounds. Art is extracted from a concept sheet: sprites are usable in the game but still need human cleanup and performance testing before a v0.1 tagged release.

## Controls

| Move | Keyboard | Touch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ollie | ↑ / W / U (tap) | U cabinet button |
| Catch rail grind | Keep holding ↑ / W / U while descending | Hold U |
| Pop off grind | Release jump while grinding | Release U |
| Double ollie | Find AIR BOOST, then tap jump again while airborne | Tap U again after pickup |
| Go faster | SPACE or A toggles, 30% ramp | A toggles speed (pressed state) |
| Future special | Not yet available | Large lightning-bolt Ø on left (reserved) |
| Pause / resume | P / Esc, pause button | Pause / Resume buttons |
| Gamepad | A = jump / hold to grind, release to pop | Standard controller |

Jump physics: gravity 1800 logical px/s², ollie impulse -620 px/s, rail-pop impulse -380 px/s. Board landing uses a forgiving rail snap zone. Input buffering is 100 ms and ground coyote time is 80 ms. A double jump is deliberately *not* always available: it is an extra power-up, avoiding a mandatory second button.

## Obstacles and scores

- **Accept All**: low pill-shaped button, clears for +3.
- **Consent popup**: floating modal with a tiny close control, +5.
- **Agree & Continue**: tall wall introduced in tier 4, +10.
- **Clause rails**: safe to skate below; land while holding jump to grind; +2 per 32px and double the distance-based clauses while grinding.
- **Fine-print gaps**: jump the black pit (reward currently +8; balance candidate).
- **Opt Out**: +10 and a one-hit shield.
- **Air Boost**: +10 and one optional second ollie.

A procedural seeded chunk scheduler reads `src/chunks.json`, starts with a guaranteed easy rail, and unlocks tiers at 20, 45 and 90 seconds. Hand-authored chunks deliberately leave recovery space; a more complete jump-arc fairness test and five-run human playtest remain required.

## Build and embed

From `arcade/`: `npm install && npm run check && npm run e2e`.

```html
<script type="module" src="./games/grind-the-terms/dist/grind-the-terms.js"></script>
<grind-the-terms skin="./games/grind-the-terms/skins/au/skin.json"></grind-the-terms>
```

`skin` is optional. Without it, the game renders accessible procedural placeholders. Invalid or inaccessible skin assets trigger `game:error` and fall back to placeholders; change the attribute to swap skins without editing gameplay. `game:skin-loaded`, `game:power`, `game:grind-start`, `game:grind-end`, `game:near-miss`, `game:over`, and `game:share` are emitted as DOM events.

**AU art is all rights reserved.** All skater and hazard frames in `skins/au/` are protected branded artwork. The open `skins/fony/` remains placeholder-only until clean, unbranded sprite art is available. Do not redistribute the AU skin under CC BY.

See `GAP-ANALYSIS.md` for the gap-by-gap status, release blockers, and next QA steps.

Sound effects are procedurally generated by Web Audio after a user gesture (disabled by default); enable with the Sound toggle or **M**. No prerecorded music is distributed.

## Rail sprite contact calibration (v0.2.2)

The AU skin declares `rail.drawTopFromGround=39`, `rail.drawHeight=40`, and `rail.surfaceCellY=53` (out of `grid.hazards.cellH=112`). The rendered rail's skateboard contact line is at Y ≈ 279.93 in the 640×360 logical playfield. Grind catch, hold, sparks, and release-pop use the SAME calculated surface. Procedural placeholder rails draw their top at Y = 280 instead of the obsolete Y = 272. Physics is independent of Fony's sprite pose/trim box.

## Flat-topped obstacles (v0.2.3)

Fony can land on, skate across, and ollie from ACCEPT, POPUP and MODAL boxes. **Hold** while landing for a grind; **release** to pop off. If the jump is released before landing, the box acts as a rideable platform and the next tap triggers a new ollie. Running into its side still causes a bail. The first consent obstacle is a 126px-wide platform introduced immediately after the practice rail. The physical contact edge is visibly highlighted on the art. Taller drone hazards and elevated rewards are future encounter work, not yet shipped.

## v0.2.4: obstacle artwork grounded to the floor

Restored the original **ACCEPT ALL** sprite with no artificial floating lime coping line. The sprite's actual face is the grindable surface. ACCEPT, POPUP, and MODAL art now anchor their visible bottoms to the floor using source-cell measurements: ACCEPT surface 45/base 92/height 72, popup 24/105/94, modal 27/101/130 (source atlas cell height 112). Rail artwork is cropped to remove unrelated debris above its bar; its coping is source y=53, leg tips y=82, render scale height 77, so the legs meet floor Y=300 and the board meets the real bar near Y=280. Physics remains independent of skin loading: placeholder and AU share identical contact geometry.

## November 3 — ZERO / ALGORITHMIC / UPRISING portrait arcade cabinet

Phones in portrait show a width-scaled 16:9 gameplay viewport ABOVE a separate retro punk control deck inside the Web Component. The large lightning-bolt **Ø** on the left is reserved (no special move yet). Smaller A and U buttons sit on the lower-right for the right thumb: **A toggles GO FASTER**, **U is jump/hold-to-grind/release-to-pop**. The new keyboard map is **SPACE toggles speed**; ↑/W/U jump. Desktop gamepad A still jumps. The deck must be included in the component's measured height, not overlap the instructions, art picker, or other page content. The mobile portrait game screen remains 16:9 for correct pixels, not stretched to 9:16.

Speed increases smoothly up to ~30% above normal over 0.45 s, returns over 0.35 s after toggling off. Grinds sustained for ~0.85 s grant a separate short 18% momentum bonus. This is an early tuning pass, not final balance. The speed state is displayed in the HUD and on the A button's pressed state.

Still required before release: trim leftover transparent edges on hazard sprites, assemble variable-length rails with true left/center/right caps and legs, tune beginner run length, check real iPhone/Android interactions, verify 320px phone widths and mobile WebKit. The Ø special is expressly future work and must not perform gameplay yet.
